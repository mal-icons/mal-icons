# Code of Conduct

mal-icons is a community project. We want issues, discussions, reviews, and
pull requests to be useful, respectful, and welcoming for contributors at every
experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient when asking questions or giving feedback.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the impact of your words.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect maintainers' time by sharing clear reproduction steps, context, and
  tradeoffs.
- Help keep discussions on-topic and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hateful language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Doxxing, publishing private information, or sustained unwanted contact.
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Any conduct that makes the project unsafe for contributors.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers
through GitHub. Use the repository's moderation tools when available, or contact
the maintainers through the mal-icons GitHub organization.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other
reasonable action to protect the project. Reports will be handled with care and
with as much confidentiality as the situation allows.
